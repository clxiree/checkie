        Before you pay anything

        💡 Ask whether bills are included. A €500 room can cost around €580–620 once electricity, water and internet are added.
        💡 A deposit (fianza) of one to two months' rent is typical. Only pay it after you've seen the room and read the contract.

        Ranges are approximate 2026 asking prices and change quickly; always compare with current listings. Sources: idealista, Q2 2026 room report · Uniplaces, Cost of living in Barcelona 2026 · CasaRadar, Barcelona rent prices 2026.

# Checkie: Scope

*Last updated: 25 October 2026. Author: Gau Yi Chi*

## Problem
International and exchange students looking for a room in Barcelona often meet rental scam warning signs. Most check a listing by asking students who went on exchange before, or seniors.

## Riskiest guess (Guess 1)
International and exchange students in Barcelona run into rental scam warning signs often enough that a quick check before paying is worth having.
**I am right if** at least 3 out of at least 10 real students say they have come across a listing with warning signs (e.g. asked to pay before a viewing, no real video, photos that don't look real).
**Status (25 October):** 12 of 15 said yes (12 Esade exchange students, 3 UPF students). Early support, not proof.

## My one goal for Session 6
One anonymous question inside Checkie that a student can answer in under 60 seconds, with no personal data.
**Status:** shown on 13 October but not saving answers; saving through Netlify Forms since 25 October.

## In scope
- 12-question scam-pattern check with a three-level result that never says a listing is safe.
- Anonymous review question (saved on the public site through Netlify Forms).
- Optional screenshots that stay on the device; AI screenshot check only in the Claude version, on the student's own Claude account.
- "Need to ask a real person?": the student's university international office contact.
- Area and price guide, campus travel check, page for universities.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Saying any listing is safe or legitimate.
- Naming or rating landlords, or storing listings, addresses or names.
- Taking money from landlords, agencies or housing platforms.
- AI check on the public Netlify site (needs a server with an API key; planned only with a partner university).
- Human review of screenshots (only with partner universities).
- Legal advice.

## Business model
The university pays an annual subscription for a co-branded link in pre-arrival and welcome emails. Students always use Checkie for free.

## AI-first vs kept for myself

**AI-first:** Almost all of the code (the SQL, the prototype, the design, the map, the travel estimates, the AI screenshot check, keeping feedback in the Claude version, the university contact card), looking up official university contacts, and first drafts of my pitch and these handouts.

**Kept for myself:** choosing the venture and the goal, deciding which features to add or cut (including overriding the AI's advice whenever necessary, and later replacing my own hackathon card with a review section), deploying and testing, giving the demo, taking in feedback given by professors during my demo and incorporating the relevant feedback and changed accordingly to make Checkie even better and the final wording of my records.

**What I learned about working with AI:** I learnt to always make my own judgement after what AI produces as it does not fully understand the vision I may have.
